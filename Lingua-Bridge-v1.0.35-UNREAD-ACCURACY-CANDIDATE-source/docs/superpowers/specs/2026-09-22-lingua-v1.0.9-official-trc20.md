# Lingua Bridge v1.0.9 Official TRC20 Release Specification

## Approved decisions

- Product: Lingua Bridge v1.0.9 Official for Windows.
- Billing network: USDT-TRC20 on TRON mainnet only for this release.
- Owner receiving address: `TC4gLVT6RnjdnMB6qCgq2mAb5KNnM9ovzM`.
- Billing periods and prices: Pro 9 USDT for 30 days; Business 29 USDT for 30 days.
- Stripe remains disabled and hidden.
- The installer offered to customers must be the verified v1.0.9 installer, not an older build.

## Customer flow

1. A customer opens the public product site, creates an account, and verifies the email address.
2. The customer opens Billing and chooses Pro or Business.
3. The server creates a pending claim containing a unique exact USDT amount and the approved TRC20 address.
4. The customer sends that exact amount on TRC20.
5. The server accepts only a confirmed, successful USDT transfer to the exact owner address, within the claim window, with a transaction hash not used by another payment.
6. A match activates the selected plan for 30 days and records `paidUntil`.
7. Failure or an explorer outage never suspends or downgrades the customer. The owner may inspect proof and manually confirm a legitimate pending payment.

## Owner flow

- `/admin` is server-side restricted to the exact `ADMIN_EMAIL` owner identity and admin role.
- The dashboard shows customer counts, emails, plans, expiry dates, payment records, and active devices.
- Gift/access codes remain separate from purchases and may be created for trials or manually granted access.
- Secrets, download tokens, API keys, and wallet credentials must never be embedded in the desktop installer or public source.

## Public distribution and search

- The public landing page is indexable and contains Lingua Bridge name, description, plan prices, system requirements, support contact, privacy policy, and terms.
- `/admin`, authenticated dashboards, billing records, and token-gated download internals are not indexable.
- `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, canonical metadata, Open Graph metadata, and structured software-application data are published.
- The customer download route delivers only the approved Windows installer and publishes its SHA-256 checksum.
- Google indexing is requested only after the production domain, public pages, and installer download pass smoke tests. Index appearance timing is controlled by Google and cannot be guaranteed.

## Release gates

- All static audits, build, database migrations, authentication, payment isolation, and download authorization tests pass.
- Production environment contains the exact approved wallet address and `CRYPTO_CHAIN=tron`.
- A fresh non-owner account completes signup, email verification, download, login, and translation smoke tests.
- One small real TRC20 payment completes end-to-end and activates the correct account for 30 days before general sales open.
- Installer code signing is strongly recommended. If unsigned, the public page must disclose the SHA-256 checksum and Windows may display a reputation warning.
